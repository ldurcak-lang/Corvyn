## English

**App name:** Corvyn

**Short description:**
Dark action RPG: five heroes, four chapters, dungeons and rifts. Plays offline.

**Full description:**
The dead of Grey Ford do not sleep. Someone woke them to bring back the Raven Crown, and you are the only one who can stand in the way.

Corvyn is an isometric action RPG in the spirit of the classic games of the early 2000s, built for phones and thumb controls.

FIVE HEROES
• The Necromancer raises skeletons and a bone golem
• The Barbarian cleaves, leaps into the crowd and grows stronger with his war cry
• The Mage commands fire, ice and lightning
• The Ogre crushes with his club and throws enemies around
• The Huntress shoots a crossbow, sets traps and fights alongside her raven

A STORY IN FOUR CHAPTERS
The Dark Forest, a cursed graveyard, the crypt of Prior Morvael, the Marshes, Raven Castle, the Frozen Pass, an old mine and finally the Raven Tower, where Morana, Lady of Winter and Death, awaits. Dozens of side quests, dialogue and an objective marker.

LOOT AND GEAR
• Common, magic, rare, unique and set items
• Legendary effects that change how you play
• Runes and sockets, reforging at the smithy, gambling with a wandering merchant
• A shared chest for all your characters

AFTER THE STORY
• The Wandering Dungeon with endless depths
• Timed rifts with rising difficulty
• A weekly boss with changing modifiers
• Nightmare and Hell difficulties, mastery from level 30
• Hardcore characters with a single life
• Online leaderboard, achievements and daily challenges

A LIVING WORLD
Day and night, elite enemies with combined powers, random roadside events and a raven that brings you gold.

• Joystick or tap controls, mirrored for left-handed play
• Plays offline, no ads and no purchases
• English, Czech and Spanish
